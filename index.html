Week rules
Left always leads. On every single-arm or single-leg movement, the left side goes first and sets the number. The right side matches it and stops — never more, even when it easily could. This is the entire mechanism for closing the gap.

No spinal loading. No deadlifts, no barbell rows, no weighted sit-ups or Russian twists, no loaded rounding of the lower back. Hip thrusts and split squats build the same muscle without compressing L4/L5.

Two pulls for every push. Rounded shoulders come from a strong front and a sleeping upper back. Face pulls and Y-raises are prescribed every session and they are the ones to never skip.

Stop two reps short. Every set, all year, except the last set of the last exercise. Grinding to failure buys nothing and costs the next session.

Eating — the part that decides it
You gain and lose fast, which means training is the easy variable. At 75 kg with a high metabolism, growing needs roughly 3,000 kcal and 150 g of protein a day, every day, including the days you don't train. Miss that and twelve months of good sessions will measure the same as six.

The simplest version that works: protein at every meal (eggs, chicken, meat, fish, labneh, cottage cheese), a real breakfast before work, and one deliberate extra — a shake, a sandwich, a handful of nuts — in the late afternoon. That last one is usually the whole difference.
